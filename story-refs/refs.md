## Patterns across all 14
- **13/14** open with "In this town…" (or "Everyone in my town…") as the very first words. The other one opens with the kid, then states the rule in sentence two.
- **Nearly all** have a kid hero, and **about 8** put a sibling, best friend or grandparent at risk.
- **About 10** use a reframe twist (the rule or the monster wasn't what the town thought).
- **Most of the long ones** pay off a small detail planted early.
- Under 60 s: horror plus a sharp one-line twist plus a loop cut. Over 90 s: an emotional arc plus a comic button. The biggest view counts are on the long, warm, funny ones.
